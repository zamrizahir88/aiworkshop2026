    Part 1: Web basics

    Why Google Sheets as our database?

    Press → to reveal

    Great for

    FamiliarEveryone already knows spreadsheets
    FreeIncluded with your UniMAP account
    VisualWatch each booking arrive as a new row
    FlexibleFilter, chart, share, export to Excel

    Watch out for

    Small scale onlyFine for a faculty, not thousands of users at once
    No sensitive dataAvoid IC numbers, grades and medical information
    Editors can change dataShare the Sheet itself carefully
    Daily quotasGoogle limits how much a script can run per day
    A big system would use a proper database like MySQL. For a faculty with a few hundred users, Google Sheets is more than enough, and you can literally watch the data arrive. Be honest about the limits. Also note: users of the web app don't need access to the Sheet. The script reads and writes it on your behalf.

    What is vibe coding?

    A term popularised in 2025 for building software by talking to an AI in plain language, instead of typing every line yourself.

    The 4 golden rules: build a prompt

    Toggle each rule, or press →
    AI
    Build Prompt 1 live, one rule at a time. Watch the prompt grow and the AI's reaction change. Role changes the vocabulary and depth. Context gives what the AI can't guess: our faculty, our 6 rooms, our platform. Task makes it specific and countable. Constraints set the rules: format, tone, and what not to do. Every missing rule is something the AI has to guess, and guesses are where mistakes come from.

    This is the dialog you'll see at 10:45. Execute as: Me, so the app uses your permission to write to your Sheet. Who has access: Anyone within Universiti Malaysia Perlis. When you first authorise, Google shows "Unverified" because it's your own personal script, not a reviewed product. That's expected for your own code. Never authorise scripts from strangers.
